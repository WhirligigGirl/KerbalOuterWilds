# Minor spoiler warning for Outer Wilds:

# Universe
* 35 billion years old
* Heading for Big Rip in 60 million years. Distant galaxies already redshifted away.
* There are no Gamma-Ray Bursts because the distant galaxies they tend to occur in are not visible, and supernovae in the galaxy are too rare. Most gamma radiation is from stars.


# Galaxy
* Small elliptical galaxy ejected from its cluster early on. Has never had any bursts of star formation, so it has slowly been using up its hydrogen and it has only been depleted in the last 5 billion years or so. There are still star forming regions, but none as big and dramatic as the bright Messier objects.
* The far edges are already experiencing Big Rip redshifting--this would be visible to the IR telescope as extremely red-shifted stars in the direction of the center of the galaxy (but the far side of it).
* Hearthians don't know what to make of this, as there are no cepheid variables with which to measure distance and, without a high-precession mercury, they haven't invented general relativity yet, so expansion of space is not even on their radar.


# Sun
* K3IV Orange Subgiant
* 20 Billion years old.
* Formed when universe was 15 billion years old.
* Previously an Orange Dwarf.
* Started to burn hydrogen in a shell around its core.

# Hourglass Twins
* Formerly habitable.
* Oceans boiled off due to increased insolation around 15 billion years of age, or 5 billion years ago, while the Sun was still main sequence.
* Spent a few billion years with a water atmosphere. Water got split into H2 and O2 by solar radiation. H2 escaped, leaving O2. 
* Ember Twin, being a little larger and dryer, remained habitable for longer, so its atmosphere remains thicker.
* Ash Twin became geologically dead before Ember Twin, and its oceans eroded away all of the underlying land.
* The flat sand on both twins are salt flats.
* Fossilized life readily found on Ember Twin, in reference to anglerfish fossil.

# Timber Hearth
* Used to be covered in polar glaciers, with all of the basin water hidden under ice. Hearthian life did not even see the Sun until a few million years ago.
* Impact basins much younger than highland terrain (by billions of years), evidence of a late period of giant impacts on Timber Hearth.
* High desert is uninhabitable to Hearthians without PPE. Air too thin, too dry. Hearthians are not aware of other impact basins, since all modern-day desert-crossing expeditions have failed.
* Each basin has been niche partitioned for literally billions of years. Most basins do not share common tree of life.

# Attlerock
* Moonlike planet. Cooled off entirely, then was hit with giant impact on farside (around the same time as the Timber Hearth basins were formed.)
* No longer in hydrostatic equilibrium. Even the spherical parts do not *quite* match the idealized ground level.
* Life: none.

# Brittle Hollow
* Surface entirely covered in columnar-jointed igneous rocks, implying past periods of heavy volcanism. Columnar joints are formed by uneven cooling of lava.
* Primarily basalt surface with bluish gem inclusions.
* Lowland terrain comprised of reddish gems.
* Dry atmosphere with only thin high altitude clouds. Transient snowfall on the poles.
* Life: Confined to microscopic gaps between ice crystals in the polar snow. Very very slow timescale.
    * Stretch goal for surface scatters: Different trees for each basin. Pine trees only in Village Basin and Canyons.

# Hollow's Lantern
* Tidal heating results in powerful volcanism and lava flooding.
* Volcanism is transient--heat builds up under the crust until it all bursts out at once. During these events, Hollow's Lantern outshines Brittle Hollow.
* Orbit is decaying, and is within a few tens of thousands of years of beginning to be pulled apart. 

# Dark Bramble
* Water planet. Rocky core with pressure-ice mantle, water ocean, and cold ice crust.
* Giant impact (same time period as Timber Hearth & Attlerock, but much more significant) restarted the clock on internal cooling.
* Chemophilic microbes utilizing chlorate respiration have resulted in a lot of chlorine gas dissolved in the ocean, which makes its way into the atmosphere and results in the yellow tint.

# Giant's Deep
* Ammonia-water oceans. Hydrogen-methane atmosphere.
* Life has panspermia'd from Dark Bramble some time after the giant impact on the latter. As a result, the atmosphere contains a trace of chlorine, giving it a cyan tint.
* Giant's Deep life uses methanogenic respiration and plants photosynthesize by the hydrogenic reaction: Methane + Water + Light-> Formaldehyde + 2 Dihydrogen. (Photosynthesis in Hydrogen-Dominated Atmospheres, 2014). Less light is needed for hydrogenic photosynthesis than for oxygenic photosynthesis on Earth, so the dim Sun and the cloud cover are not an issue.
* Perchlorates in the surface and oceans.
* Formaldehyde--a class 1 carcinogen--in large quantity in the oceans.
* Traces of hydrochloric acid in oceans.
* Trees: PVC plastic instead of cellulose.
* Clouds: Ecosystem of photosynthesizing microbes in the clouds give it its greenish color.

Stretch goal: Outer Wilds narrative content.

# Eye of the Universe
* Alien megastructure transmitting knowledge of the early history of the universe, in all directions.
* Information like "there used to be other galaxies," the W value of the expansion of the universe, general relativity, etc.
* Intended to last until the end of time, but a malfunction has corrupted some of its data.
* Stranger inhabitants and Nomai both visited the Solar System in an attempt to recover the data.
* Hearthian predict an object of its mass at roughly its distance but have not discovered it yet.

# Quantum Moon
* Knowledge bank carrying the data being transmitted by the alien megastructure. Uses quantum communication to transmit its data to the Eye.

# The Stranger Inhabitants
* Aliens from a star system that once neighbored the solar system who sent robotic spacecrafts to explore the Eye of the Universe and attempt to recover the missing information. When it transmitted the recovered data, they decided to attempt to deactivate the link between the Quantum Moon and the Eye of the Universe. They built a quantum interference device to block the quantum communication between the two objects. The Nomai became aware of the Eye under the same circumstances as in the original game.

# The Stranger
* Sun-decelerated laser sail spacecraft with a digital simulation of Stranger Inhabitants running on computers inside. It is possible to take a science report from the Stranger that indicates you have entered the simulated world, but that an Inhabitant kills you before you can do anything. 

#The Nomai
* Only encountered the Eye signal very briefly, enough to tell that the signal was without any doubt artificial, but unable to determine what the nature of the signal was. The Nomai explored the Solar System hoping to find the source of the transmission or the civilization that was responsible, but found nothing. A navigational error while exploring Dark Bramble led to the Vessel entering the atmosphere. The escape pods ended up on Ember Twin and Brittle Hollow. Possible homeworld swap patches for Nomai Space Program?
